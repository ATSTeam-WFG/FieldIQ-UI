## Activties

Users log activities when engaging with real estate agents or any other title deal related contact. The activties are usually covered by the users (title agents in our case) by default but some times we can have sponsors being involved in the activity who might fully or partially sponsor the activity. 

---

### Default Logging

The following details should be logged by default when logging in activity

- Contact: The person with whom or for whom the activity was conducted
- Date
- Time
- Cost
- Notes
- Receipt (image or pdf)

### Type of Activities

1. Lunch: Users will have lunches with contact. The logging of the following information can be done when logging this activty

2. Pop Bys: Users will pop by at agent's office to discuss more details.

3. CE Class: The users can provide CE Classes to the agents for intuitive learning and education

4. Coffee: The user's can meet the agents for coffee to discuss contract details, etc

5. Closing Gift: The users usually offer gifts to the agents on closing contracts or successful deals. 

6. Call: Logging of important meetings and calls with is also something that the users can do.

7. Other: Custom activity specific to users are also possible.

### Follow Ups

Every activity might have a discussion regarding a follow up meeting with the agent. The details of the time and date for this meeting are discussed between the two parties, and should be logged as an additional information in every activity optionally so the system can notify the user with a reminder in future.
