# FieldIQ

## What Problem Are We Solving?

Title agents win business the same way they always have — through relationships. The lunch, the pop-by, the CE class, the closing gift. These touchpoints are the actual work of field sales in the title industry. But none of it gets recorded anywhere.

Closing platforms like Qualia and SoftPro capture deals after the fact. They tell you what closed, not how it was earned. A manager can see that an agent had a bad quarter, but by then it's already too late — the pipeline dried up weeks ago, silently, because no one was watching the activity that feeds it.

Enterprise CRMs like Salesforce weren't designed for this. They're built for generic B2B sales cycles, not for the specific rhythms of a title agent managing a territory. They're expensive, bloated, and agents don't use them because they don't feel built for the way they actually work.

The result: agents fly blind on their own relationships, managers coach to outcomes they can't influence, and agencies lose deals to competitors who are simply more present and more consistent.

---

## What Is FieldIQ?

FieldIQ is a relationship intelligence platform built specifically for title professionals.

It turns the invisible work of field sales — every meal, every visit, every call — into structured data that agents can act on and managers can trust. It answers the questions that matter most: Which relationships are going cold? Is my team doing enough to earn the pipeline we expect? Who needs coaching, and on what?

FieldIQ is not a generic CRM. It is purpose-built for the title industry, with workflows designed around how title agents actually work.

## Who It's For

### The Field Agent — Sarah Chen, Senior Title Agent, Buckhead Territory

Sarah spends her days outside the office. Lunches with real estate agents, pop-bys at closing offices, co-sponsoring CE events, dropping off closing gifts. She manages relationships with dozens of referral agents and sponsors across her territory.

She has no system of record for any of it. She can't tell you which relationships she's been neglecting, how her spend is tracking against her budget, or whether she's on pace for the month. She finds out a deal went to a competitor not because she didn't know the agent — she did — but because she hadn't been in front of them in two months without realizing it.

**What she needs:** A fast way to log what she did today. Visibility into which relationships are healthy and which are cooling off. A clear sense of whether she's on track — before the month is over.

---

### The Sales Manager — Jane Doe, Regional Sales Manager, Premier Title Agency

Jane manages a team of agents across a region. She can see deal counts. She cannot see anything else.

By the time pipeline dries up, it's already too late to intervene. She finds out when closings drop, not when agent activity drops — weeks after the moment she could have done something about it. Her 1:1s are based on whatever her agents tell her, which means she's coaching on vibes rather than evidence.

**What she needs:** Early warning signals — activity slowdowns before they become pipeline problems. A fair way to compare agent effort, not just outcomes. Data that makes her coaching conversations specific and actionable instead of vague.

## What FieldIQ Offers

### For Agents

**Activity Tracking**
Log every client touchpoint with a few taps — lunch, pop-by, CE class, coffee, closing gift, call, sponsorship, or anything custom. Record who it was with, what it cost, when it happened, and any notes worth keeping. Attach a follow-up task so the momentum from a good meeting doesn't get lost. Mark an activity as sponsored when a partner co-funded it.

**Relationship Intelligence**
Every contact in your book gets a Relationship Score from 0 to 100, computed across four dimensions: recency (how recently you engaged them), frequency (how often), diversity (how varied your touchpoints are), and engagement (how strong the relationship signal is). The score tells you instantly which relationships are healthy and which are quietly going cold — before you lose the deal.

**Contact Management**
A full relationship book covering referral agents, sponsors, lenders, and attorneys. Each contact carries their tags, your total spend with them, how many closings they've referred, and a complete timeline of every interaction. Know exactly where every relationship stands without having to dig through your memory or your inbox.

**Contract Pipeline**
Track title orders from introduction through closing. Log the deal, link it to the referring contact, and follow it through status milestones — initiated, pending, updated, closed. Know what's in play, what's stalled, and what just closed at any point in the month.

**Follow-Up Management**
Every pending follow-up in one place, organized by urgency: overdue, this week, upcoming. Connect to your calendar. Mark things complete or cancel them cleanly. Stop letting follow-ups die in a sticky note or a notes app you don't open.

**Personal Performance**
Your own scoreboard for the month, quarter, or year. Activities logged, total spend, contacts touched, how many days in a row you've logged something. See whether your trajectory is on pace for your targets while there's still time to do something about it.

---

### For Managers

**Team Performance Overview**
A live leaderboard of every agent on your team, rankable by activity volume, spend, relationship score, or how recently they logged. Switch between month-to-date, quarter-to-date, and year-to-date in one click. See the whole picture at once, not just deal counts.

**Early Warning System**
Automated alerts surface when an agent hasn't logged in days, a follow-up is overdue, or activity is tracking below target. Get ahead of the problem instead of reacting to it after the pipeline has already thinned.

**Activity Intelligence**
Understand how your team actually spends their time and budget — broken down by activity type across the whole team. See whether agents are diversifying their relationship-building or defaulting to the same one or two things week after week.

**Agent Deep Dive**
Pull up any individual agent and see their complete picture: recent activity, spend by type, relationship score, pipeline health, performance vs. target. Walk into a 1:1 with specific data. Ask about the right things. Coach to behaviors that actually move outcomes.

## Why Now

Title agencies are under margin pressure. Competition is tighter. Every dollar of agent spend needs to show a return. The agencies that win are the ones whose agents are more consistent, more present, and more deliberate about the relationships they're building.

There is no purpose-built tool for this today. Agents use spreadsheets. Or notes apps. Or nothing. FieldIQ fills that gap with something that actually fits how title professionals work — not something repurposed from another industry and squeezed to fit.
